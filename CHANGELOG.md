### Added

  turn-taking MLP and ASR-backed TrustGate.

  no-speech handling.

  ASR window.

### Preserved

- All GRPO sweep results, including negative MiniCPM-o 9B runs.
- Baseline mode when TFD runtime environment variables are unset.
